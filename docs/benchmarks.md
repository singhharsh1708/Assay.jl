## Gradient cost by backend and dimension

Forward mode costs one pass per parameter and wins while that number is small. Enzyme is reverse mode and overtakes it early; the crossover here is below ten parameters, and by two hundred it is nearly two orders of magnitude ahead. Finite differences are the reference the gradient tests check against, not a serious option.

| dimension | ForwardDiff | ReverseDiff | Enzyme | finite differences |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0.5 us | 3.3 us | 1.0 us | 1.2 us |
| 10 | 1.1 us | 23.0 us | 1.5 us | 7.6 us |
| 50 | 63.1 us | 414.9 us | 5.8 us | 159.5 us |
| 200 | 4105.0 us | 7289.3 us | 64.2 us | 7490.6 us |
